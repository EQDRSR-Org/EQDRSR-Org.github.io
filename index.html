Hemlock Ecosystem Restoration Project (HERP)

EQDRSR is restoring a 1.5‑acre creekside hemlock ecosystem along Mineral Creek, removing trash, healing the land, and sharing what we learn through educational books and media so others can help rescue Biosphere Earth.

Hemlock Ecosystem Restoration Project (HERP)

Location: Mineral Creek, North Carolina (creekside, 1.5 acres)

Origin: Removal of 5 trailer‑loads of trash from the creekside to prepare the land for rescued hemlocks.

Goals:

  • Restore: Clean, stabilize, and re‑green the creekside habitat.
  • Rescue: Protect and replant hemlocks and companion species.
  • Educate: Document the process so others can replicate ecosystem restoration.

Activities:

  • Trash removal and debris clearing along Mineral Creek.
  • Hemlock rescue, planting, and monitoring.
  • Soil, water, and shade management for long‑term health.
  • Photo, video, and written documentation for public education.

HERP is the heart of EQDRSR’s current mission: hands‑on restoration plus simple, shareable lessons for anyone who wants to heal their own patch of Earth.

Educational Books – Funding & Teaching the Mission

Mission Harmony – The Race to Rescue Biosphere Earth

Author: Equine
Focus: A narrative and educational journey about rescuing Biosphere Earth, connecting personal action to planetary restoration.

Proceeds: Book proceeds are dedicated to EQDRSR and the Hemlock Ecosystem Restoration Project, helping fund tools, plants, and documentation.

It Only Gets Better (IOGB)

Status: In development – second educational book.
Focus: How small, persistent actions and community‑scale projects make life and ecosystems better over time.

IOGB will expand the educational side of EQDRSR, offering hope, practical steps, and stories from HERP and other restoration efforts.

Programs

  • Hemlock Ecosystem Restoration (HERP): Core field project on Mineral Creek.
  • Environmental Education: Books, articles, and media explaining ecosystem restoration in simple, actionable language.
  • Mission Harmony & IOGB: Publishing and outreach to support EQDRSR’s work.
  • Legacy – Equine Disaster Relief & Search and Rescue: Historical origin of EQDRSR; equine rescue and SAR experience that shaped the organization’s values.
